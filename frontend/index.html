ABOUT GO TRIP

A simple place to plan your next Indian adventure.

We are designing Go Trip around one basic rule: a traveller should understand where they are going, what the package includes, how much it costs and what happens after booking.

✓ Destination, duration and starting price shown clearly
✓ Booking information designed to stay easy to find
✓ Company and support details kept visible

Frequently Asked Questions

Where can I see my booking after I book?

After login, the final version will provide a My Bookings section with booking ID, travel date, travellers, payment status, cancellation and ticket download.

Will I receive a booking confirmation?

Yes. The frontend is prepared for a confirmation screen and, later, email/PDF confirmation from the Spring Boot backend.

Can I cancel a booking?

The final booking system will show the applicable cancellation policy and allow eligible bookings to be cancelled from My Bookings.

How do I contact Go Trip?

The Contact section will show the company's verified phone, email, office address and support hours once those real business details are configured.
